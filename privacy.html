Privacy Policy for Vaulty Last Updated: August 30, 2026 Welcome to Vaulty ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclosure, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension and our website. 1. Information We Collect We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when using Vaulty: Account & Authentication Data: If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and password credentials to manage your authentication state via Firebase Authentication. Clipboard Content & Web History: When you copy text while using the extension, Vaulty captures the copied text snippet, time of capture, and the source webpage URL. Storage Data: Your clipboard history is saved locally on your device (chrome.storage.local) and, if you are logged in, synchronized to a secure Cloud Firestore database under your account document. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected information solely for the single purpose of providing and maintaining Vaulty's functionality: To allow you to store, search, and manage your copied clipboard history. To synchronize your clipboard snippets across your active browser sessions and VDI environments. To manage your account session (sign-in, sign-up, and password resets). We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers. 3. Storage and Third-Party Services Vaulty utilizes trusted third-party infrastructure to provide service features: Firebase (Google Cloud): Used for user authentication and Cloud Firestore database storage. Your data is stored securely using Google Cloud infrastructure. Local Browser Storage: Used to provide offline accessibility on your local machine. 4. Data Retention and Deletion You can delete individual clipboard snippets or clear your entire vault at any time through the Vaulty extension popup. Deleting items removes them from both local storage and Cloud Firestore immediately. Logging out detaches cloud synchronization and switches Vaulty to offline-only mode. 5. Contact Us If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please reach out to us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
